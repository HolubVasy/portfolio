Case 04 · Public sector, customs · first rollout 2018, medal 2019

Customs inspection paperwork, from my own idea to a state medal

Organization  National customs serviceRole  initiator and developer of the system

Proposed and built a web system for customs inspection paperwork. A document now takes 5 to 7 minutes instead of 10 to 30, data comes from central reference lists instead of being retyped, and the system earned a state medal.

Challenge

I filled in customs inspection documents myself and saw how much time they took and how often they contained errors. The same data had to be typed again for every document. The head of the customs office supported the idea of entering it once.

Constraints

The system had to pass state certification, including security certification. The customs service officially supported only Oracle, so the system moved from MySQL to Oracle, and I prepared 300 pages of certification documentation.

Decisions
01
A web system with a central database

Instead of extending the old local program, which had no central database, no shared reference lists and no control over who submits what.

02
Present the system to top management as a security measure

Instead of presenting it as faster paperwork and lower inspection costs. All data in one database, and no submissions by people without the right to make them.

Results
From 10 to 30 min down to 5 to 7
time to prepare one inspection document, from the logs of the old and the new program
About 15 customs posts
using the system, rolled out with on-site training
State medal, 2019
for ensuring economic security, awarded for this system
Takeaway

Work with the manager who knows how to present an idea to the people who make the decision.
