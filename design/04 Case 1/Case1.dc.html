Case 01 · IT services, internal employee portal · 2025

Accounting integration without portal downtime

Company  IT services company, under NDARole  .NET developer, de facto lead of the .NET part

Replaced a nightly full-rewrite sync that took the portal down for 20 to 30 minutes with a background delta sync. Hourly updates and no downtime.

Challenge

The internal employee portal, used by about 5,000 people, took its data from the company's accounting system. The director of internal projects asked for fresher data, and with the old integration that was impossible.

Starting point

The integration pulled all data from the accounting system, stopped the portal, rewrote the whole database and started the portal again. Users worked with yesterday's data.

Decisions
01
One generic comparison engine driven by attributes

Instead of hand-written comparison code for each of the 30 entities, which would also have to be maintained one by one.

02
Build the comparison in-house

Instead of a third-party reconciliation tool, which meant a dependency on a third system and its maintenance.

Results
From 1 to 24 syncs a day
sync frequency
From 20 to 30 min to zero
portal downtime per sync
From 20 to 30 min to 10 to 15
one full sync run
About a year
stable in production after release
Takeaway

A solution's cost does not end at delivery. Given the choice between 90% building and 10% maintaining, or the other way round, I choose the first.
