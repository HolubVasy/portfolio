03 · How I work

Four rules I keep, each learned on a real project

01

A solution's cost does not end at delivery

Given the choice between 90% building and 10% maintaining, or the other way round, I choose the first. Learned on the accounting integration, Case 1.

02

Show early, ask early

A prototype in front of people earlier beats a polished version later. Learned on the Grafana dashboards, Case 3.

03

Big changes in one branch, tested where it looks like production

A cross-cutting change lives in one branch and is checked in an environment that matches production, and the rest of the team knows it is coming. Learned on the cache removal, Case 2.

04

Find the person who knows how to present the idea

Talk to the manager who knows how to frame an idea for the people who make the decision. Learned on the customs system, Case 4.
